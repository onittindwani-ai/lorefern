# 03 Clients and Customers

## What belongs here
One brief per client, service levels, onboarding, standard agreements.

## What does not belong here
Invoices, payment details, or private talk about a client.

## Who owns it
The account manager owns this folder at {{COMPANY}}. The owner keeps every document here current and answers questions about it.

## How often it is reviewed
This folder is reviewed quarterly. The review date of each document sits in its header. A document past its review date is flagged in the weekly operating review.
