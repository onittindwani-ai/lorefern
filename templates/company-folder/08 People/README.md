# 08 People

## What belongs here
Leave, reviews, hiring, onboarding, salary bands.

## What does not belong here
Anything about one person that the whole company may read.

## Who owns it
The people lead owns this folder at {{COMPANY}}. The owner keeps every document here current and answers questions about it.

## How often it is reviewed
This folder is reviewed every 6 months. The review date of each document sits in its header. A document past its review date is flagged in the weekly operating review.

## Who may see it
Visibility tag: people. Every document here takes this tag, or a narrower one when its own header says so (`visibility: inherit` means "take the folder's tag"). A document can never be seen by more people than the folder allows.
