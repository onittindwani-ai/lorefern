# 00 Governance

## What belongs here
How the company is run: operating rules, document standards, decision records, and who owns what.

## What does not belong here
Day to day work notes, client material, anything private about a person.

## Who owns it
The founder owns this folder at {{COMPANY}}. The owner keeps every document here current and answers questions about it.

## How often it is reviewed
This folder is reviewed quarterly. The review date of each document sits in its header. A document past its review date is flagged in the weekly operating review.

## Who may see it
Visibility tag: company. Every document here takes this tag, or a narrower one when its own header says so (`visibility: inherit` means "take the folder's tag"). A document can never be seen by more people than the folder allows.
