# 11 Templates

## What belongs here
Blank templates: SOP, decision record, meeting note, client brief.

## What does not belong here
Filled in documents. Copy a template, then save the copy in the right folder.

## Who owns it
The operations lead owns this folder at {{COMPANY}}. The owner keeps every document here current and answers questions about it.

## How often it is reviewed
This folder is reviewed yearly. The review date of each document sits in its header. A document past its review date is flagged in the weekly operating review.

## Who may see it
Visibility tag: company. Every document here takes this tag, or a narrower one when its own header says so (`visibility: inherit` means "take the folder's tag"). A document can never be seen by more people than the folder allows.
