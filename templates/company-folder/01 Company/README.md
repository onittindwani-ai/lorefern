# 01 Company

## What belongs here
Who we are: profile, values, team directory, history, locations.

## What does not belong here
Pricing, salaries, or client specifics.

## Who owns it
The founder owns this folder at {{COMPANY}}. The owner keeps every document here current and answers questions about it.

## How often it is reviewed
This folder is reviewed every 6 months. The review date of each document sits in its header. A document past its review date is flagged in the weekly operating review.
