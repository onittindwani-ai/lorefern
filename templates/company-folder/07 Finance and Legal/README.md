# 07 Finance and Legal

## What belongs here
Budgets, spending rules, payroll summaries, contract notes, insurance.

## What does not belong here
Anything staff need to see. Put policies everyone follows in 05 Operations.

## Who owns it
The finance lead owns this folder at {{COMPANY}}. The owner keeps every document here current and answers questions about it.

## How often it is reviewed
This folder is reviewed quarterly. The review date of each document sits in its header. A document past its review date is flagged in the weekly operating review.

## Who may see it
Visibility tag: finance. Every document here takes this tag, or a narrower one when its own header says so (`visibility: inherit` means "take the folder's tag"). A document can never be seen by more people than the folder allows.
