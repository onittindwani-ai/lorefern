# What each page does

| Page | What it is for |
| --- | --- |
| **Ask** (and Cmd K anywhere) | Ask a question. You get an answer, the passage it used with the document title and section, and a button to open the document at that spot. A question the documents cannot answer gets "not found". Two documents that disagree are both shown, never silently merged. |
| **Tasks** | A board of Proposed, Open, In progress, Blocked and Done. Proposed tasks came from meetings and wait for a person. Open a card to approve, reject, change the owner or add an update. Drag a card to move it. Every change can be undone for a short time. A table view handles thousands of rows. |
| **Meetings** | Each transcript with its action items and decisions marked in the text. Import a transcript by pasting it. Lines that read like orders to the system are shown struck through and are never acted on. |
| **SOP map** | Work the company repeats (seen in at least 3 meetings over 2 weeks), coloured by whether a written procedure covers it: covered, needs review (old or past its review date) or no procedure. Click a tile to see the evidence. |
| **Weekly review** | A document built by code from last week's tasks, decisions and meetings. There is a company version and a leadership version. Every line has a link, and the page tells you how many lines are linked. |
| **Decisions** | What was decided, by whom, why, and the meeting and words it came from. A decision found in a transcript waits to be confirmed. |
| **Sources** | Where Lorefern reads from, who may see each source, and whether the index is current. The owner can change a source's visibility and read it again. |
| **Company folder** | The folder Lorefern learns from, as a tree, with the template check results (missing owners, missing review dates, broken links). Owner and leadership only. |
| **Integrations** | Every connector, what it needs, and a Test button. |
| **Approvals** | Requests that need a second person, for example sending reminders. The person who asked cannot approve their own request. |
| **Audit log** | Every change, who made it and when, chained so an edit after the fact shows. |

## The banner

- **Demo mode** means sample data and offline tools.
- **Draft mode** means reminders are drafted and never sent.
- **Live** means reminders can go out, but only after another person approves them.
- **Kill switch on** means nothing is sent and no change is saved (approvals, imports, edits, reading sources, the background jobs) until the owner turns it off. Asking questions still works.
- **Previewing as** means the owner is looking through a narrower audience's eyes. A preview cannot change anything.
