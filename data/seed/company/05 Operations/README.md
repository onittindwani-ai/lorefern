# 05 Operations

## What belongs here
How recurring work is done: SOPs, policies, checklists, meeting notes.

## What does not belong here
One off projects and client specific files.

## Who owns it
The operations lead owns this folder at Pinegate Works. The owner keeps every document here current and answers questions about it.

## How often it is reviewed
This folder is reviewed every 6 months. The review date of each document sits in its header. A document past its review date is flagged in the weekly operating review.
