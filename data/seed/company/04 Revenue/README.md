# 04 Revenue

## What belongs here
Pricing, terms, the sales process, proposals and win or loss notes.

## What does not belong here
Salaries, costs, or payroll.

## Who owns it
The account manager owns this folder at Pinegate Works. The owner keeps every document here current and answers questions about it.

## How often it is reviewed
This folder is reviewed quarterly. The review date of each document sits in its header. A document past its review date is flagged in the weekly operating review.
