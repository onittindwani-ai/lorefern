# 06 Product and Engineering

## What belongs here
How the product or service is built and run: runbooks, release steps, the tech stack, backups.

## What does not belong here
Credentials, keys or passwords. Never store secrets in a document.

## Who owns it
The lead engineer owns this folder at Pinegate Works. The owner keeps every document here current and answers questions about it.

## How often it is reviewed
This folder is reviewed every 6 months. The review date of each document sits in its header. A document past its review date is flagged in the weekly operating review.
