# Lead handling SOP

1. Every web lead gets a first response within 15 minutes during office hours.
2. Try to reach the lead 3 times over 2 days: phone, text, then email.
3. Log every attempt in Fieldwise, our CRM and job system.
4. After 3 attempts without an answer, mark the lead Cold. Do not delete it.
