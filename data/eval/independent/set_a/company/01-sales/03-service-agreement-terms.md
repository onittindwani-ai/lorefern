# Service agreement terms

Jobs over $3,000 need a deposit of 20 percent before work is scheduled. If a customer cancels within 48 hours of the start date, the deposit is not refunded. Quotes stay valid for 30 days. Change orders are written, signed and priced before the extra work starts.
