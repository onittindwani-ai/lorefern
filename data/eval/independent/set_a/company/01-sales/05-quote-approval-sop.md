# Quote approval SOP

Who may approve a quote before it goes to the customer:

- Under $5,000: the estimator (Ptolemy).
- $5,000 to $15,000: the operations manager (Dev).
- Over $15,000: the owner (Marguerite).

Every quote needs the site photos attached. Permit filing starts only after the customer signs.
