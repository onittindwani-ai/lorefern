# Residential price list 2026

Effective 1 February 2026.

- Diagnostic visit: $89. If the customer approves the repair, the diagnostic fee is applied to the repair bill.
- Hourly labor: $128 per hour.
- After-hours and holiday work: 1.5 times the normal labor rate.
- Trip fee: $0 within 25 miles of the yard, then $2.10 per mile beyond that.
- Quotes are valid for 30 days.
