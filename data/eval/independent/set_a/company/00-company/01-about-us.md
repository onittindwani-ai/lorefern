# About Harbor and Pine

Harbor and Pine Heating, Cooling and Plumbing was founded in 2011 by Marguerite Okonkwo-Hale in Alderford. We have 24 employees and a fleet of 14 vans. We serve Alderford County and the three towns just across the river. Our head office and yard are at 410 Quarry Street. Our promise is simple: arrive inside the 2 hour window and leave the house cleaner than we found it.
