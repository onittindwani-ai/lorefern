# Emergency callout SOP

An emergency is: no heat when the outside temperature is below 40 degrees F, a burst or actively leaking pipe, or a gas smell. For a gas smell the customer must call the gas utility first.

- The on-call technician must respond to the customer within 60 minutes.
- The on-call rota rotates every Monday at 8:00.
- The on-call stipend is $350 per week.
- After-hours labor is charged at 1.5 times the normal rate.
