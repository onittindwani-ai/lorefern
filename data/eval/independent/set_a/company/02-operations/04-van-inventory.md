# Van inventory standard

Every van carries at least 2 complete 3/4 inch copper fitting kits and one spare 40 gallon water heater anode rod set. Vans are restocked at the yard every Thursday. Dev counts inventory on the first Monday of each month. The stock value limit is $6,500 per van.
