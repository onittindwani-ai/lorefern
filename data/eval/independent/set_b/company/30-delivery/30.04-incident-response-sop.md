# Incident Response SOP

Severity 1: acknowledge within 15 minutes and post an update every 30 minutes. Severity 2: acknowledge within 1 hour. A postmortem is due within 5 business days of any Severity 1 or 2 incident. The incident lead is the on-duty engineer.
