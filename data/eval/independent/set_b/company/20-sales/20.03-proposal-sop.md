# Proposal SOP

1. A proposal is sent within 3 business days of the final scoping call.
2. Any proposal worth 100,000 euros or more needs CEO sign-off before it is sent.
3. A proposal is valid for 45 days.
4. Every proposal states the delivery phases and the named principal.
